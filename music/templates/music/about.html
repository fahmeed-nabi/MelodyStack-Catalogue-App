About This Project

Music Media Storefront is a team project developed for CS 3240: Software Engineering as part of the Cataloging and Lending Apps (CLA) initiative. This application allows users to create and share collections of media items—such as CDs, vinyl records, cassettes, and more—within a shared lending environment. Users can organize items into collections and borrow items, while librarians can create items and collections and manage them.
